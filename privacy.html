Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

KonuşUp! is an app for young children that supports speech development. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does not.

Summary

  • The app does not send the child’s name, voice or progress to the developer or to third parties. Only for subscriptions, a randomly generated app user ID and purchase details go to RevenueCat.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • The child’s voice is never recorded or sent. Speech recognition runs only on the device.

Information handled on the device

  • Child profile: the name and avatar a parent enters.
  • Learning progress: completed lessons, stars, time played and the day’s words.
  • Parent settings: microphone permission status and parent-area choices.

This information is stored in the app’s own storage on the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used to check whether the word the child says is the right one. Recognition runs only on the device; audio is not sent to any server and is not stored. A parent can turn off microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

Camera and photos

The app does not use the camera or the photo library.

Subscriptions and third parties

Subscription payments are processed by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) verifies the subscription status. It receives a randomly generated app user ID and the purchase history (product, date, renewal state). This is not linked to the child’s name or voice and is not used for advertising or tracking. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or social-network libraries.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 0–5. No personal data is knowingly collected from children. The parent area and the purchase screens sit behind a parental gate.

Your rights and contact

To delete the data on the device, delete the app. For questions, open a support request. Data controller: Ege Yergün.
